We are excited to announce the first official public release of **AudioPad**! AudioPad was built out of frustration with existing bloated, ad-ridden, and paid subscription soundboards. It is completely free, open-source, and engineered for speed.

AudioPad lets you play sound clips directly through your microphone during voice calls on Discord, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and inside online games — with near-zero latency.

## Key Features

- **Low-Latency Audio Routing**: Built with a high-performance C++ native backend, keeping playback latency under 20ms so your audio cues land right on time.
- **Simultaneous Headphone & Mic Output**: Hear sound clips through your own headset in real time while feeding clean audio into your virtual microphone.
- **Global Hotkey Support**: Assign keyboard shortcuts to any sound and fire them off seamlessly even while running full-screen games.
- **Lightweight Desktop UI**: Modern web-based GUI running within a memory-efficient native WebView container.
- **Cross-Platform Installers**: First-class packaging for Windows (Inno Setup `.exe`) and Linux distributions (`.deb`, `.rpm`, `.AppImage`).
- **Privacy & Open Source**: No accounts, no data collection, no ads, and no locked paywalls. 100% free under the GNU GPLv3 license.
